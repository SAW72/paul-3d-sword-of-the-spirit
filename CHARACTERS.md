# Character List – Realistic Edition

All characters are photorealistic cinematic portraits designed for a modern youth game (ages 8-15).

## Playable / Main

### Young Disciple (Level 1)
- ~15-year-old Jewish believer
- Curly dark hair, intense eyes, simple tunic
- The player’s first avatar – hunted, quick feet, growing faith

### Ananias of Damascus (Level 2)
- Middle-aged disciple, gray-streaked beard, kind but resolute face
- The man who risks everything to obey God and heal Saul

### Saul of Tarsus → Paul the Apostle
- **Young Saul**: Fierce, dark-haired Pharisee with soldiers behind him
- **Mature Paul**: Balding, thick beard, weathered traveler holding a scroll
- **Final Paul**: Silver-haired, radiant, crown of righteousness above him

### Simon Peter (Level 5)
- Older fisherman-apostle, gray beard, calloused hands, humble yet strong

## Supporting
- **Saul’s Guard** – Fully armored Roman-style temple soldier with torch and spear (stealth enemy)
- **Lame Man of Lystra** – Full scene of the healing moment
- **Armor of God visualization** – Epic spiritual warrior scene for Ephesus level

## Art Style
AAA cinematic realism (Uncharted / Assassin’s Creed / The Chosen hybrid).  
Dramatic lighting, highly detailed skin & fabric, emotionally powerful faces that kids and teens actually want to look at.

All generated with Grok Imagine and integrated into the game.
