## Identity
~15-year-old Jewish believer. Hunted, quick feet, growing faith. First player avatar. Intense, determined expression — not child-cute, not adult.

## Face & hair (from portrait)
- Olive / warm Mediterranean skin, smooth youth
- Large intense brown eyes, thick dark brows
- Full lips, strong jaw for age
- **Curly dark brown / black hair** — tight curls, full volume on top and sides; slight beard stubble / short chin hair OK (portrait shows early facial hair)
- Nose: straight, strong bridge

## Body
- Lean adolescent male proportions (not bulky)
- Height ~1.65 m
- Athletic but not gym-built; runner’s legs
